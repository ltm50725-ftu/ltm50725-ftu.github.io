Course Project

AuctionHub

Group of 3 | Semester 1, 2026-2027 | Foreign Trade University

A prototype multi-category auction website for C2C trading in Vietnam, covering the full journey from live bidding to order tracking, with separate Buyer, Seller and Admin roles.

  • Auction flow: search and filters, countdown timer, minimum bid increment, bid history and watchlist.
  • Order flow: simulated checkout, shipping tracking and refund requests, synchronised between Buyer and Seller.
  • Role-based access with protected routes for Buyer, Seller and Admin.
  • Full project documentation: business plan (Lean Canvas, SWOT), product requirements, UX/UI design and technical roadmap.

  • Vite
